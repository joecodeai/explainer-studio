## A. The story in one sentence
A hustle guru insists that getting up at 4 a.m. is why he's rich. His routine is so hollow that even his reflection walks out, he fires his own kidney to cut down on sleep, and then we learn his "4 a.m. sunrise" is a desk lamp filmed at 4 p.m.

## B. Why this is the strongest introduction
- **The hook carries the premise.** The first 4 seconds state it: "It's four a.m. Right now, you're asleep. That's why you're broke." Anyone who has seen a guru video gets it at once.
- **One character, one claim.** Every moment tests Brayden's "4 a.m. = success" claim, and each beat makes the claim more absurd. Nothing is a random clip.
- **The ending pays off the opening.** The lamp reveal makes the hook funnier in hindsight: the sunrise he was filming in front of was fake.
- **It holds back most of the episode:**
  - Gerald's weekend-subscription sketch
  - the gym and side-hustle numbers
  - the sleep science
  - the bomber/survivorship story
  - the FTC course schemes
  - Ryan's butler story and the verdict
- **The question stays open.** "So, is hustle culture a scam?" is asked on screen and never answered. That's the teaser; no "watch the full video" line is needed.

Captions always sit in the band under the picture, at the same height, clear of the bottom interface and the right-side buttons.

## H. Additional work
None: zero new dialogue, voice or animation. Two pipeline changes:
- New tool `tools/shorts/promo.py`. It does per-frame vertical composition (full-width blur pad, width-fraction boxes, eased pans and push-ins, sequential sub-spans) and reads each shot from its own segment file, so no frames bleed in from neighbouring shots.
- Better caption word timing in `build.py`: when whisper writes "4" for "four", the words are now aligned by sequence instead of being spread evenly.

## Final QC
- **One story?** Yes. One guru, one claim (4 a.m.), each beat escalating it, and the ending exposing it.
- **Every cut makes sense?** Yes:
  - The routine leads to the mirror ("tell the mirror", then the mirror leaves).
  - "Team" leads to the organs.
  - "Kidneys" leads to a kidney leaving, then "He fired a kidney."
  - "four a.m. videos?" leads to the lamp.
- **Satisfying ending?** Yes. The lamp reveal reverses the hook, then the fox is the actual 4 a.m. riser (a callback to shot 1).
- **Makes a stranger want the episode?** The question "Is hustle culture a scam?" is posed and left open. The episode's Gerald, statistics and verdict are untouched.
- **Anything cut off?** No:
  - The swan is whole in the close framing.
  - Mirror, boardroom, lamp-room and fox shots are full frame.
  - Captions never cover a face or prop.
- **Audio:** a whisper transcript of the final file matches the script line for line, with no clipped words. A no-VAD pass "heard" words over the fox ambience; with VAD it found no speech, so it was a hallucination on the alarm and street sound.
