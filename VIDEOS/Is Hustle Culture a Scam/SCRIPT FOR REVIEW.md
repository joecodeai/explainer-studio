## PART B: THE FINAL SCRIPT, IN THE 7-PART FORMAT

### 1. VIDEO CONCEPT
- **Topic:** hustle culture.
- **Central argument:** hard work isn't a scam. The scam is the packaging: exhaustion sold as a personality, survivors sold as proof, and insecurity sold back by subscription.
- **Comedic angle:**
  - Brayden is the true believer.
  - Gerald is the system that profits.
  - Ryan is the tired person both of them sell to.
  - Maya is the friend who has seen through all of it.
- **Why it's interesting:** it's the sequel to the channel's best performer ("Is Working 9 to 5 a Trap?"), with the same audience and the next question.

**Markers:**

| ⏱ | Animation-only hold, counted in the runtime |
