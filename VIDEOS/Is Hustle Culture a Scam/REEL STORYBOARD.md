# Hustle Culture Reel: single-story concepts and storyboard (for approval, NOT rendered)
This follows the 2026-11-25 rule: one complete situation, story first, runtime second. Every duration below comes from the real timeline of the finished episode.

## Three single-story concepts

### 1. "HR Took My Weekend" (Gerald and Ryan, one meeting) — RECOMMENDED
- **Story:** HR's Gerald calmly talks Ryan out of his Saturday, sells it back to him as a monthly subscription, and then reveals the only way to cancel.
- **Source:** the whole scene w01–w16 (01:15–02:34), in its original order, with nothing skipped.
- **Runtime:** about 79 s (or about 70 s without the cemetery offer; see the notes below).
- **Continuity:** the strongest of the three. It's one room, two people and one continuous conversation that already exists as a single scene.
  - Every shot is either the next line of the conversation or a reaction to it.
  - The ending ("Cancellations are done in person. On a Saturday.") pays off the very first ask ("Saturday. You don't need all of Saturday. Do you?").

### 2. "Brayden's 4 A.M. Morning" (Brayden alone)
- **Story:** a guru's legendary 4 a.m. routine turns out to be a routine for filming his routine, and his "sunrise" is a desk lamp at 4 p.m.
- **Source:** f04, d01–d04 (02:58–03:25), then v05–v09 (08:52–09:08).
- **Runtime:** about 42 s, or 54 s with the cold-open video c01 as the hook.
- **Continuity:** good inside the morning-routine section. But there's one real jump: from the routine (03:25) to the lamp reveal (08:52). It also has a contradiction: c01 shows him filming outdoors beside a real car, while the reveal says his sunrise is a lamp indoors. A stranger could fairly ask "wait, which video?"

### 3. "Brayden Restructures His Body" (Brayden and his organs, one meeting)
- **Story:** Brayden holds a team meeting to cut sleep. The team is his organs. He promotes the heart, puts the liver on a performance plan and fires a kidney, and finally his brain walks out.
- **Source:** b02–b09 and b12–b15 (05:00–06:12). The science card b10/b11 is left out.
- **Runtime:** about 49 s.
- **Continuity:** strong, one meeting. But its middle is a list (heart, then liver, then kidney) rather than a developing situation. Its payoff (the brain asleep with a bin bag) needs Maya's science line b13 to make sense. That works, but it's a lecture in the middle of the sketch.

**Why 1 wins:**
- It's the only one that is already a complete scene with a beginning, a turn and an ending, in one place, with nothing missing in between.
- It has a clear want: Ryan wants to keep his weekend.
- It has an opponent who keeps escalating:
  - asks for Saturday
  - asks for the afternoon
  - takes it
  - sells it back monthly
  - discontinues the old plan
  - offers a cemetery
  - makes cancelling require a Saturday
- It ends on a punchline that only works because of the opening line.

It was also our best-received character (Gerald) in your notes. The earlier Short 2 used this scene, but it was trimmed mid-speech, dropped six moments and was centre-cropped. This version is the whole scene, uncut, at its natural pace.

## Notes and choices for you
1. **Length:** 79 s. If you want it shorter, the clean cut is shots 13–14 (the cemetery offer), giving about 70 s. Shot 15 would then be reframed on Ryan's face only, to hide the brochure he's holding, which would otherwise be unexplained. I recommend keeping it: the cemetery is what finally makes him ask to cancel.
2. **The door (shot 4):** the "no handle" detail never rendered; the door has a handle. It still works as Ryan's glance at the exit, because Gerald's next line explains it.
3. **Background colour shifts:** in shots 3, 10 and 16 the background warms or cools during the line (orange to teal). This is in the long video too and reads as mood lighting, but you'll see it.
4. **Specks:** the image model's small coloured specks (found today across our videos) appear in this scene on shots 2, 5, 7, 13, 14 and 15. The Reel will clean them frame by frame.
5. **Ending:** no title card and no "watch the full video". It ends on Gerald's smile. The upload caption names the episode. If you'd prefer a 3 s "Is hustle culture a scam?" card after the hold, say so.
