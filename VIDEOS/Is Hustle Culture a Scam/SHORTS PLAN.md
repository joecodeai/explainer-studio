### 1. He Gets Up at 4 AM. Or Does He *(mini-story, about 48 s)*
- **Story:** a man who swears he's up at 4 a.m. shows us his routine, which turns out to be filming it. Then we learn his 4 a.m. was 4 p.m., and he isn't bothered at all.
- **Problem:** Brayden wants us to believe discipline is why he's rich.
- **Escalation:** his "routine" is a book about reading, then his reflection walking out on him, then filming, editing and posting the routine.
- **Payoff:** the sunrise is a desk lamp, filmed at four in the afternoon. "Still counts." The opening ("It's four a.m.… that's why you're broke") becomes funnier in hindsight.
- **Hook (0–2 s):** Brayden, at dawn beside a sports car, shouting into his phone: "Good morning. It's four a.m."

### 2. HR Took My Weekend and Sold It Back *(mini-story, about 53 s)*
- **Story:** HR's Gerald gently talks Ryan out of his weekend, sells it back to him as a subscription, and explains that cancellations are done in person. On a Saturday.
- **Problem:** Ryan wants to keep his weekend, then wants out.
- **Escalation:** "Saturday… you don't need all of Saturday", then "Put the weekend down, Ryan. Slowly", then he hands it over, then it comes back in tiers, "billed separately", "And now you can have it back. Monthly."
- **Payoff:** "How do I cancel?" "Cancellations are done in person. On a Saturday." The ending lands on the exact day the story started with.
- **Hook (0–2 s):** a smiling HR man with a box of tissues: "I'm Gerald, from People and Culture. Nobody's in trouble."
- **Changes from the long video:** "Hi, Ryan." is trimmed from the first line, and the "Weekend Lite" sentence is dropped. Both cuts are at sentence ends and change no meaning.

### 3. He Fired His Own Kidney *(visual story, about 49 s)*
- **Story:** a sleep-hating guru holds a 4 a.m. board meeting with his own organs, until his brain makes the decision for him.
- **Problem:** Brayden wants to cut sleep to get more done.
- **Escalation:**
  - his heart is promoted until its desk vibrates away
  - his liver goes on a performance plan
  - a kidney is made redundant: "He fired a kidney."
  - "Sleep is a mindset."
  - the brain drags its own rubbish out ("researchers think" sleep clears waste from the brain)
- **Payoff:** the brain lies down across the doorway and the lights go out, body by body. The body forces the sleep he banned.
- **Hook (0–2 s):** a boardroom table of cartoon organs in lanyards, followed straight away by "Team. Let's talk about sleep."
- **Order change:** the organ-table reveal is moved in front of Brayden's speech, which gives a stronger first frame.

### 4. The Iceberg of Failed Hustlers *(comedic explanation, about 43 s)*. Not made.
- **What it is:** survivorship bias, from the yacht on the iceberg tip to thousands waving underwater, Ryan and his own-face mugs, and Wald's bombers. It ends on "Although honestly, I'd listen."
- **Why it's not in the top 3:** the hook is a question ("why does any of this feel true?"), which is slow for a Short. Its best laugh (Ryan's mugs) is a callback that needs knowing Ryan.

### 5. Twenty-Seven Percent Broke Even *(comedic explanation, about 30 s)*. Not made.
- **What it is:** multi-level marketing, from the candle woman's group chat emptying (her mum leaving last), to the Inner Voice's "Text your ex. Sell her a candle", the AARP figures, and "So a quarter of them did unpaid work." / "Enthusiastically."
- **Why it's not in the top 3:** it's strong but short, and the statistic does most of the work, so it's less of a story.

## 2. EDITING DOCUMENT (exact, from the real timeline)
**Applies to all three Shorts:**
- **Aspect:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps.
- **Framing:** character shots are a 9:16 crop of the 16:9 frame, centred unless an x value is given. Wide gags (the mirror, the hand-over, the organ table, the heart's desk, the kidneys, the lamp reveal, the sofa) are the full frame over a blurred, enlarged copy, so the joke's object is never cropped out.
- **Subtitles:** Montserrat ExtraBold 84 px, white with a black outline, 2–3 words at a time, centred, about 600 px above the bottom. That keeps them clear of the app's bottom bar, and the side margins keep them clear of the right-side buttons.
- **Sound:** the long video's own mix (voices, ambience and sound effects), with 30 ms crossfades at each cut, normalised to −14 LUFS. No music.
- **Silence:** every written-in hold from the long video is kept ("Still counts", "He fired a kidney", lights out, "On a Saturday").
- **Ending:** no call to action. Each Short ends on its punchline or reveal.
