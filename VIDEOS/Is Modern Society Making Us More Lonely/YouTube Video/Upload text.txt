TITLE
We Can Message Anyone on Earth. So Why Are So Many of Us Lonely?

DESCRIPTION
We have more ways to talk to each other than at any point in history, and yet Britain and Japan both appointed ministers for loneliness. Is modern society really making us lonelier? We look at both sides: the Surgeon General's warning and the WHO's global numbers, vanishing time with friends, living alone and Bowling Alone, the smartphone debate between Jonathan Haidt and his critics, and why researchers disagree on whether loneliness has actually risen. Then: what the Harvard Study of Adult Development, a train full of Chicago commuters and a Dutch supermarket suggest actually helps.

If you're feeling lonely right now, you're far from the only one. Reaching out to a friend, family member or doctor is a good first step.
