## Why this story
- **One problem:** Ryan pays for things he can't use. It escalates: he can't cancel, and he can't even die.
- **The hook is real and surprising:** BMW heated seats for £15 a month. Ryan's radiator line makes it personal.
- **The payoff calls back to the opening:** the fox ends up in the warm seat Ryan won't pay for, for free.
- **Held back for the episode:**
  - Future Ryan
  - the Iliad/Amazon story
  - HP ink
  - Brayden owning nothing
  - the pet carrier ("It gets Sunday evenings")
  - the verdict

## Options
- **The pet carrier:** "It's been asking about you." / "Is it eating?" / "It gets Sunday evenings." That's +6 s (about 60 s total), and it's the episode's best visual gag. I left it out to keep the Reel tight and save it for the video.
- **The call-to-action wording** can be anything you like. It's the only new recording.
