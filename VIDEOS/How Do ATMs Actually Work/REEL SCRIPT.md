## Why this story
- **One situation:** Brayden's ATM, from buying it to paying himself. Each step causes the next:
  1. He buys it.
  2. Nobody comes.
  3. He has to fill it with his own money.
  4. He pays a truck to bring it back.
  5. He withdraws it and loses $1.64.
- **The payoff is a real mechanic:** the owner's fee goes to him, but his own bank's out-of-network fee doesn't. The $1.64 is the real 2025 US average (Bankrate).
- **The hook is a surprising fact** (anyone can own an ATM) with a character attached.
- **Held back for the episode:**
  - the bath and the radioactive cheques
  - the four-digit PIN and the cup of tea
  - Gerald in Authorisations and the $14
  - Antarctica
  - skimmers and jackpotting
  - the fox inside the machine
- **Left out to stay under about 60 s:** Ryan's pizza visit (seven lines). It is a side trip from Brayden's story, so the story still holds without it.
