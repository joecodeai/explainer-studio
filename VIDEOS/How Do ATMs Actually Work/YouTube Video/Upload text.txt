TITLE
How Do ATMs Actually Work? (The Wall That Gives You Money)

DESCRIPTION
You put plastic into a wall, type four numbers, and the wall gives you money. We followed one withdrawal across the world: why the machine doesn't know your balance, why your PIN is four digits (one man's wife, one cup of tea), why the first cash machine was mildly radioactive, what that $4.86 fee is for, and why typing your PIN backwards won't call the police. Gerald has moved to Authorisations. Brayden has bought an ATM. The fox is inside it.
