TITLE
How Insurance Actually Works (It's a Bet You Want to Lose)

DESCRIPTION
For eleven years, Ryan has paid a stranger forty-five dollars a month in case something terrible happens. Nothing has. He's furious. We followed the money from a village bucket to a skyscraper: how insurance really works, how insurers make money by investing your premiums while they wait, why we overpay to avoid small bills, and why a claim can cost you even when it's approved. Gerald has moved to Claims. Brayden has started his own insurance company. The fox is doing very well.
