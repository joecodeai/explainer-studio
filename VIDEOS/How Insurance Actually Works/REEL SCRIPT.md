## Why this story
- **One situation:** Ryan's claim. Each line makes it worse, and the payoff is a real mechanic: the damage is under the deductible, and claiming can still raise your price.
- **The hook is relatable:** you pay for years and nothing happens, and Ryan is annoyed about it. Then the flood is a guilty "Finally."
