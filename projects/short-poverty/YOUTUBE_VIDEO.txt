TITLE
Are Poor People Poor Because of Bad Decisions?

DESCRIPTION
In America, a bank can charge you $35 for not having enough money. So are poor people poor because of bad choices, or because being poor is unbelievably expensive? We audit both sides: the "success sequence", the marshmallow test and what it really measured, the scarcity experiments with New Jersey shoppers and Indian sugarcane farmers, payday loans and the poverty premium, where you're born, Raj Chetty's Moving to Opportunity research, and what happens when you just give people cash. Then: the honest verdict.
