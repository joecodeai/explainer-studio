TITLE
If a Robot Could Do Your Job for Half the Price, Should Your Boss Hire It?

DESCRIPTION
If a machine can do a job for less, why pay a person? It sounds like simple maths, but it's one of the biggest arguments of our time. We look at both sides: power looms and cash machines, the Luddites and the hand-loom weavers, Klarna's AI experiment, and what the research actually says. Then: cheaper for whom?
