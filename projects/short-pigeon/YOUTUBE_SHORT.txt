- America Built a Pigeon-Guided Missile. It Worked.
- 3 Pigeons Inside a Missile (Project Pigeon, 1943)

DESCRIPTION
In WWII, psychologist B. F. Skinner trained pigeons to steer a missile by pecking at the target. Three birds per missile, in case one got distracted.
It worked in testing. Nobody took it seriously. Then the Navy brought it back anyway.
