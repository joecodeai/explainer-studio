TITLE
Why Is Everything a Subscription Now? (Even Your Car Seat)

DESCRIPTION
BMW once charged British drivers fifteen pounds a month to switch on a seat heater that was already in the car. We looked at why so much of life is now rented monthly: the subscriptions we forget, the future self we sign up for, and the exits built to be just annoying enough. Gerald has moved to Retention, and he'd love a quick word before you go.
