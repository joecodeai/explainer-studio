TITLE
The UK Just Won the 2035 Women's World Cup. It Once Banned Women's Football.

DESCRIPTION
FIFA has confirmed England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland as hosts of the 2035 Women's World Cup. A century ago, the same football authorities banned women from their grounds.

This video covers the Dick, Kerr Ladies, who drew 53,000 fans in 1920, and the FA's 1921 ban. It covers FIFA's first Women's World Cup in 1991, which was officially called "for the M&M's Cup", and the 2023 record crowds. It ends with what the 48-team 2035 tournament means for the future of women's football.
