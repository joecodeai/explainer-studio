TITLE
Is Marriage Becoming Obsolete? (The Numbers Are Surprising)

DESCRIPTION
Four in ten Americans say marriage is becoming obsolete. Yet people still spend the price of a car on one afternoon to do it. We look at the numbers, the surprisingly unromantic history of marriage, the case that it's dying, the case that it isn't, and what it's turning into.
