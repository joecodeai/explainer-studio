TITLE
Keynes Promised Us a 15-Hour Week. Is the 9 to 5 a Trap?

DESCRIPTION
In 1930, John Maynard Keynes predicted his grandchildren would work fifteen hours a week. Most of us hit fifteen hours by Tuesday afternoon. So is the nine-to-five a trap? We look at where the forty-hour week actually came from (Henry Ford, the 1938 Fair Labor Standards Act), Parkinson's law, the commute, Gallup's engagement numbers, the UK and Iceland four-day week trials, and how hours differ between countries. Then the other side: business failure rates, sick pay, health insurance, and what a 1930s Austrian village found happens when the work disappears. Then an honest verdict.
