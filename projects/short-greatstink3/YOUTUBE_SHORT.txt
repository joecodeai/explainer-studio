DESCRIPTION
In the summer of 1858, the River Thames smelled so bad that Parliament couldn't stand it. A sewer plan ignored for years was approved in 18 days.
Parliament ignored it for years. Then they had to smell it. The sewers they built to fix a smell ended up helping protect London's drinking water.
