# History & educational story slate (21 Nov 2026)

**What's working (vidIQ, last 6 months):**
- "Biggest idiots in U.S. history who turned stupidity into disaster": 466k views (227×) on a 2.5k-sub channel.
- "Historical coincidences so strange they sound made up": 203k (65×).
- Loophole stories: Lume's "Pepsi spent $2 million to stop one kid" got 1.7M, and "He legally cheated American Airlines out of $21M" got 418k.
- Bounties that backfired: "The British paid people to kill cobras" got 974k (23×) with a 3-minute video.
- Little-known history days: "Britain skipped the clocks going back (1968)" got 93k (415×) on a 608-sub channel.

**Why these suit Ryan & Maya:** almost every one is a *plan* that goes hilariously wrong, or works too well. That's Ryan's whole character. Ryan schemes and Maya knows how it ended.

**My picks, in order:**
1. **#1, the 1904 marathon.** Funniest true story, open lane, rock-solid sources, and very visual (car, dogs, rotten apples, brandy).
2. **#2, Hanoi rats.** Educational and a big proven hook, and the "the cobra story isn't proven" angle makes us more accurate than the competition.
3. **#3, Dagen H.** Unique, visual, and almost no one has done it as a story.

#4 + #5 make a strong "loopholes" follow-up pair.
