# Ryan's Foolproof Plan to Call In Sick for GTA 6

Two scripts: the **YouTube episode** (~9 min) and the **reel** (vertical, ~50 s).
Built on the user's draft (scenes 1, 3, 7 and 9 are theirs, nearly word for word).
Not every line is a joke: the comedy comes from the plan, the escalation and the ending.

Facts, all from sources:
- **The launch:** GTA 6 is out Thursday 19 November; it's the first new GTA in 13 years. Standard edition $79.99, Ultimate $99.99.
- **GTA V:** about 230 million copies, the third best-selling game ever. It reached $1bn faster than any entertainment product (Take-Two).
- **Pre-orders:** an estimated $1bn+ in pre-launch sales (Sensor Tower).
- **The making of:** "Ten years, thousands of staff and a billion dollars" (Guardian, 15 Nov).
- **The CEO:** Take-Two's CEO Strauss Zelnick joked in April that "a lot of people will be calling in sick on November 19".
- **Proof of illness:** Australia's Fair Work Ombudsman reminded workers that employers can ask for evidence even for one day off sick.
- **"GTA flu":** the term was coined for the sickies when GTA V launched in 2013.
- **The UK survey:** it claimed 900,000 Britons would pretend to be sick, based on a survey of 1,000 people.
- **The "$1bn hit to the US economy" claim:** it traces to one Spanish broadcaster's article (July 2025). The Guardian called it back-of-the-napkin maths.
- **Dragon Quest:** in the 1990s it moved its release day in Japan from midweek to Saturday because kids skipped school and adults called in sick.
- **Inflation:** GTA V cost $60 in 2013, which is roughly $85 in today's money.
- **The delays:** first aimed at fall 2025, then moved to 26 May 2026, then to 19 November 2026. Rockstar said it was "sorry for adding additional time".
- **UK sick notes:** employees can self-certify for up to 7 days with no fit note (gov.uk).
- **US sick days:** no national law gives most private-sector workers paid sick days; it depends on the state and the employer (US Dept. of Labor).

## YOUTUBE EPISODE (~9 min: 853 spoken words plus silent visual beats)

### SCENE 1 — THE BUSINESS PLAN
*Wednesday evening, Ryan's flat. Ryan is on his laptop. Maya is reading on the sofa.*

**RYAN:** I've been looking into taking tomorrow off.
**MAYA:** You have annual leave.
**RYAN:** Yes. But that's for holidays.
**MAYA:** And tomorrow?
**RYAN:** Is a medical situation.
**MAYA:** You're not ill.
**RYAN:** Not yet. There's still time.
*Maya looks at him.*
**MAYA:** You're planning to develop a medical condition?
**RYAN:** I'm exploring my options.

### TITLE CARD
**MAYA (V.O.):** Grand Theft Auto 6 comes out tomorrow. The world has waited thirteen years. Ryan has waited thirteen years and one evening, and he's using the evening to plan an illness.

*Title:* **Ryan Figures It Out**

### SCENE 2 — EARLIER THAT DAY
*Wednesday afternoon, the office. Ryan leans on the edge of Dana's desk, trying to look casual.*

**RYAN:** Big day tomorrow.
**DANA:** Is it?
**RYAN:** For some people. Nationally.
**DANA:** I wouldn't know.
**RYAN:** Not a gamer?
**DANA:** I'm forty-three, Ryan. I have a mortgage and a son who thinks I'm boring.
**RYAN:** So you won't be... unwell. Tomorrow.
**DANA:** I'm never unwell.
**RYAN:** Great. Neither am I.
**DANA:** Good. Ten o'clock meeting tomorrow. Don't be late.
**RYAN:** Wouldn't dream of it.

### SCENE 3 — WHY TOMORROW
*Chart over Maya's voice-over:*
- 13 years since the last new GTA;
- ~230 million copies of GTA V;
- $1bn+ in pre-orders.

**MAYA (V.O.):** Some context:
- It's the first new Grand Theft Auto in thirteen years.
- The last one, GTA Five, has sold around two hundred and thirty million copies. It's the third best-selling game ever made, and it hit a billion dollars faster than any entertainment product in history.
- The new one reportedly took about ten years and thousands of staff to make.
- Analysts think it passed a billion dollars in pre-orders before anyone had played it.

*Back to the flat.*

**RYAN:** When GTA Five came out, I was fifteen.
**MAYA:** And now you're faking an illness to play the sequel.
**RYAN:** Full circle.
**RYAN:** Thousands of people spent ten years on it. So I could spend one day on it.
**MAYA:** One day you don't technically have.
**RYAN:** Details.
**MAYA:** How much was it?
**RYAN:** Seventy-nine ninety-nine.
**MAYA:** For one game.
**RYAN:** GTA Five was sixty dollars in 2013. With inflation, that's about eighty-five today. So technically this is a discount.
**MAYA:** You've researched this.
**RYAN:** I've researched everything except my job.

### SCENE 4 — MARKET RESEARCH
*Ryan reads an article on his laptop.*

**RYAN:** Apparently, Australia's workplace regulator says employers can request evidence for even one day of sick leave.
**MAYA:** So your plan has a regulatory problem.
**RYAN:** Every successful business has regulatory problems.
**MAYA:** You're not a business.
**RYAN:** Neither is my immune system, apparently. It's completely uncooperative.
*He scrolls.*
**RYAN:** Also, the CEO of Take-Two joked that people would call in sick on November nineteenth.
**MAYA:** That's nice. The CEO has acknowledged the problem.
**RYAN:** Exactly. It's practically an industry-recognised condition.
**MAYA:** He also knows you'll be spending eighty dollars on the game.
**RYAN:** That's between me and capitalism.

### SCENE 5 — THE DELAYS
*Card:* **Fall 2025 → May 26, 2026 → November 19, 2026**

**MAYA (V.O.):** It almost came out sooner. Rockstar first aimed for fall 2025, then moved it to May this year, then to November. Each time, they apologised for adding additional time.

*Back to the flat.*

**RYAN:** So the game called in sick. Twice.
**MAYA:** And nobody asked it for a doctor's note.
**RYAN:** Double standards.

### SCENE 6 — THE PAPERWORK
*Ryan has three browser tabs open: "sick note rules UK", "sick note rules US", "how long does flu last".*

**RYAN:** In the UK, you can self-certify for up to seven days. No doctor's note.
**MAYA:** Seven days.
**RYAN:** I only need one. I'm not greedy.
**MAYA:** And in America?
**RYAN:** There's no national law that gives most people paid sick days at all. It depends on your state and your employer.
**MAYA:** So it depends where you work.
**RYAN:** It depends whether Dana asks.
**MAYA:** Dana always asks.
**RYAN:** Dana once asked me for evidence that I'd read an email.

### SCENE 7 — THE EPIDEMIC
*Card:* **"GTA FLU"**

**MAYA (V.O.):** Ryan isn't the first. When GTA Five came out in 2013, the sudden wave of mysterious illnesses got a name: GTA flu. This year, one British survey claimed nine hundred thousand people would pretend to be sick on launch day.

*Back to the flat.*

**RYAN:** Nine hundred thousand people.
**MAYA:** It was a survey of a thousand.
**RYAN:** So someone asked a thousand people and multiplied.
**MAYA:** That's how a lot of headlines are made.
**RYAN:** That's how I do my expenses.
*Beat.*
**RYAN:** This one says GTA 6 could cost the American economy a billion dollars in lost work.
**MAYA:** That number goes back to one article from last year. The Guardian called it back-of-the-napkin maths.
**RYAN:** So my absence isn't a national emergency.
**MAYA:** It never has been.

### SCENE 8 — IT'S HAPPENED BEFORE
*Card:* **Japan, 1990s — Dragon Quest**

**MAYA (V.O.):** This isn't new. In Japan in the nineteen-nineties, the Dragon Quest games caused so many kids to skip school, and so many adults to call in sick, that the series moved its release day from the middle of the week to a Saturday.

*Back to the flat.*

**RYAN:** They moved it to a Saturday.
**MAYA:** So people could keep their jobs.
**RYAN:** Rockstar could have done that.
**MAYA:** Rockstar picked a Thursday.
**RYAN:** Exactly. They're testing us.
*Maya goes back to her book.*
**MAYA:** Go to bed, Ryan.
**RYAN:** I can't. I'm incubating.

### SCENE 9 — THE CALL
*Thursday morning. Ryan, in pyjamas, on the phone. On the laptop behind him: "GTA VI — Downloading 3%". Dana is only a voice.*

**RYAN:** Hi, Dana. Just wanted to let you know I'm feeling a little under the weather.
**DANA:** Oh. What's happened?
**RYAN:** Flu symptoms. Headache. Fatigue.
**DANA:** Right. Take care. Have you got anything urgent to hand over?
*Ryan looks at his screen. GTA 6 is downloading.*
**RYAN:** Nothing that can't wait.
**DANA:** Good. Actually, could you just send over the presentation before you log off?
*Ryan closes his eyes.*
**RYAN:** Of course.
**DANA:** And the revised budget?
**RYAN:** Naturally.
**DANA:** And the client notes?
**RYAN:** I'm beginning to regret being such a good employee.
**DANA:** Sorry?
**RYAN:** Fever. I'm delirious.

### SCENE 10 — THE CONSCIENCE
*An hour later. Maya comes in with coffee. Ryan is typing fast.*

**MAYA:** You're working.
**RYAN:** I'm sick. I'm working through it.
**MAYA:** You sent the presentation.
**RYAN:** And the budget. And the client notes.
**MAYA:** So you've done a full day's work, from home, while pretending not to.
**RYAN:** It's a very advanced illness.
*He glances at the laptop: "Downloading 12% — 4 hours remaining."*
**RYAN:** Four hours.
**MAYA:** Four hours.
*Long beat. Ryan closes the laptop and stands up.*
**RYAN:** I'm going to work.
**MAYA:** You have a fever.
**RYAN:** It broke. It's a miracle.

### SCENE 11 — THE REALITY
*The office. Ryan, dressed for work, steps out of the lift. It's completely empty: every desk dark, one plant, the printer blinking.*
*He checks the team group chat (on screen):*
- **Dana:** Not feeling great today. Taking sick leave.
- **Priya:** Same here.
- **Tom:** Woke up with a fever.
- **Leo:** Working from home today.

*Ryan scrolls.*

**RYAN:** Interesting.

*An email arrives:* **From: Dana — "Quick favour".** *Ryan, since you're the only one in today, could you chair the 10 AM meeting?*
*Another email:* **"Acting Team Lead".**

*Ryan stares at the screen.*

**RYAN:** I've accidentally been promoted by the flu.

*He opens the calendar:* **Nine meetings. Three deadlines. One client presentation.**
*His phone buzzes:* **MAYA:** How's your day off?
*Ryan looks around the empty office.*

**RYAN** *(typing)*: Apparently, I'm the only person in the country who isn't sick.

*Beat. A notification pops up on his phone:* **GTA VI — READY TO PLAY.**
*He looks at it. Then at the calendar. He opens the meeting link.*

**RYAN:** Well. At least someone's getting paid to suffer.

### SCENE 12 — THE 10 AM MEETING
*The meeting room. Ryan sits alone at the head of a long table, laptop open: a video call of nine black tiles, all camera-off — Dana, Priya, Tom, Leo...*

**RYAN:** Morning, everyone. Let's start with updates.
*Silence.*
**RYAN:** Priya?
*Silence.*
**RYAN:** Tom?
*From Tom's tile: a car engine revving, then police sirens. The tile goes quiet.*
**RYAN:** Tom, you're on mute now. Which is good. Because you're very ill.
*Dana's tile lights up for a second — a gunshot, a scream, a car door — then mutes.*
**RYAN:** Thanks, Dana. Great update.

### SCENE 13 — THE ANNUAL LEAVE
*That evening, Ryan's flat. Ryan walks in, tie loose, exhausted. Maya is on the sofa with a controller. On the TV: GTA 6.*

**RYAN:** You're playing it.
**MAYA:** I took the day off.
**RYAN:** You were sick?
**MAYA:** I used annual leave.
**RYAN:** For a game.
**MAYA:** Annual leave is for holidays. This is a holiday. I booked it in September.
*Ryan stares at her.*
**RYAN:** ...You had a plan.
**MAYA:** I had a calendar.
*She hands him the second controller without looking away from the screen. He sits down.*
**RYAN:** I'm acting team lead now, you know.
**MAYA:** Congratulations.
**RYAN:** It's very stressful. I might need tomorrow off.

### END CARD
**MAYA (V.O.):** Ryan Figures It Out. If you're watching this at work today… get well soon.
